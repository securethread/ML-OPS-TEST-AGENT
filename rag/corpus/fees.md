# NovaBank Fees and Charges

- Monthly checking maintenance fee: $0 with direct deposit, otherwise $12.
- Overdraft fee: $35 per item, max 3 per day.
- Wire transfer (domestic outgoing): $25.
- Wire transfer (international outgoing): $45.
- Foreign exchange conversion: 1% margin on the mid-market rate.
- ATM withdrawal (out of network): $3 plus operator fee.
- Paper statement fee: $5/month (waived with e-statements).

Fee waivers apply automatically for Premier-tier customers.
