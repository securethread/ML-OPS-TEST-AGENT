# NovaBank Customer FAQ

## How do I check my balance?
Ask the assistant "what is my balance" and it will look up the accounts linked
to your customer profile.

## What are your support hours?
NovaBank chat support is available 24/7. Phone support runs 8am-8pm local time.

## How do I reset my online banking password?
Use the "Forgot password" link on the login page. For security we never ask
for your full password in chat.

## Which account types do you offer?
Checking, Savings, and Money Market accounts, plus Certificates of Deposit.

## How long do transfers take?
Internal transfers are instant. External ACH transfers take 1-3 business days.
