# NovaBank Lending Products

## Personal Loans
Fixed-rate personal loans from $2,000 to $50,000. APR 7.99%-19.99% based on
creditworthiness. Terms of 24-60 months.

## Mortgages
30-year and 15-year fixed, plus 5/1 and 7/1 ARMs. Pre-approval available
online. Current sample 30-year fixed rate: 6.5% APR.

## Credit Cards
NovaBank Cashback card: 2% on everything, no annual fee. NovaBank Travel card:
3x points on travel, $95 annual fee.

## Business Lines of Credit
Revolving lines from $10,000 to $250,000 for qualifying businesses.
